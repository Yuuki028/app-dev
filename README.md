#  Puss in Boots
## Puss in Boots is a 2011 American animated comedy adventure film produced by DreamWorks Animation. The film follows the origin story of Puss in Boots, a Spanish-speaking anthropomorphic cat who is a fugitive on the run from the law.
## “This is the story of a cat who became a hero. An outlaw dedicated to justice. And a lover of beautiful women. A great, great lover. Really, it is crazy. I am Puss in Boots! And my name would become a legend.”
